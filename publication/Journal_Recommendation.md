# Publication assessment

The paper can reasonably be submitted as a transparent retrospective evaluation after author-specific details and declarations are completed. Its strengths are denominator clarity, frozen analyses, preserved unfavorable external findings, and reproducible tables and figures. Its main editorial weakness is modest methodological novelty. Its principal scientific limits are poor external sensitivity, only 17 external test cases, modified labels, unresolved cross-resource identity independence, and an exploratory post-test measurement correction. These limits are stated in the manuscript rather than concealed.

Positive clinical and statistical feedback is useful but does not establish journal acceptance or clinical efficacy. Strengthening the study with a larger untouched external cohort could materially improve its prospects. If speed is essential, the current manuscript can proceed to author review and submission without changing the already examined test results into confirmatory evidence.

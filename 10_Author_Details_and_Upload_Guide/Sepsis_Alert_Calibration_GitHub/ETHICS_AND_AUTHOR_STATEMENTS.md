## What the ethics item means

It records whether review is required for this particular secondary analysis,
or whether an applicable rule or authorized determination establishes that it
is exempt or outside the scope of human-participant review. It does not mean
that new patients must be recruited or that this project received funding.

The analysis reuses the PhysioNet Challenge 2019 release and eICU-CRD public
demo with pinned YAIB-derived demo labels. No patients were recruited,
contacted or treated. The investigator did not receive an identity-linkage key.
An approval or consent waiver for the source database is not automatically an
approval issued to this researcher for the present study.

Elsevier asks for an ethics statement for human data; if approval is not
required or an exemption has been granted, authors must explain the basis.
The exact institutional and journal requirements must be checked. A new
committee approval is not asserted here, and is not necessarily needed for
public de-identified secondary data.

Suggested first step: ask a former UET supervisor or the research office who
can provide the applicable policy or a written determination for this analysis.
As an independent researcher, the author can also ask the target journal what
documentation it expects for these two public datasets. A doctor's positive
scientific review is not itself an ethics committee determination.

The first sentence is supported by the project. The final sentence is still a
completion marker. Replace it with the actual determination and reference,
or a policy-grounded explanation accepted by the target journal. Do not add an
approval number, exemption, consent waiver or institutional authority that
does not exist. When writing the final statement, distinguish a finding that
review is not required from an exemption or consent waiver actually granted.
